```
🏰 Defend Your Dungeon! A Korean Mythology Tower Defense RPG

Dungeon Guardian is a tower defense RPG inspired by Korean traditional mythology and legends.
Collect and evolve 117 guardian monsters — Dokkaebi, Gumiho, Mountain Gods, Sea Gods and more —
to defend your dungeon against endless waves of invaders!

━━━ KEY FEATURES ━━━

👹 117 Guardian Monsters
Collect creatures from 8 tribes: Dokkaebi, Gumiho, Mountain Deity, Sea God,
Underworld, Mask Dancer, Celestial, and the Eight Legions.
Each monster has unique passive abilities and a 3-branch skill tree.

🏰 Strategic Dungeon Building
Place battle rooms, magic circles, traps, and treasure vaults across 12 grid slots
to build the ultimate defense line. Placement strategy determines battle outcomes.

⚔️ 6 Chapters · 52 Stages
From peasant hordes to knight brigades, wizard battalions and holy paladins —
face increasingly powerful invaders. Epic boss battles await at each chapter's end.

🌟 Deep Progression
• Wisdom Tree: Permanently boost your dungeon with Soul Crystals
• Skill Trees: 9-node specialization paths for each monster
• Fusion System: Combine monsters to birth rare new beings
• Summon Altar: Recruit new monsters via normal/special summons

🎨 Rich Collection System
55 achievements · Monster Codex · Season Banners · Cosmetic Skins

📱 Mobile-Optimized
✅ Full offline play — no internet required
✅ No ads, no in-app purchases
✅ Portrait mode, one-handed play
✅ iPhone & iPad supported
```
